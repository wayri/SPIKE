## Initial prompt

The key requirement is SEAMLESS LETTERFORM/WAVEFORM INTEGRATION: a recognizable capital S and an electrical signal spike must be the very same geometry, not an S with a pulse attached to its top or side. The spike must shape the central silhouette and negative space of the S itself. The top and bottom curves are beautifully restrained, with coordinated radii and open counters; sharp center contrasts with smooth ends. Aim for the conceptual sophistication of integrated waveform-letter monograms, but create an ORIGINAL S shape: do not reproduce Neuralink's N, loops, path or proportions. Avoid the previous approach of three separate ribbons with a little hump on top. One unified clean geometric mark; the silhouette must remain legible as S. Flat turquoise/teal line on matte deep graphite rounded square, optional very short amber segment precisely at the central impulse only, no separate amber symbol. Generous 23 percent margins, impeccable optical balance, crisp vector-like construction. Modern, elegant, serious instrument-software identity. No ECG medical sequence, separate heartbeat, atom, chip, flame, lightning bolt pictogram, letters or labels beyond the S monogram. No texture, glow, gloss, gradients, 3D or shadows. Produce one square app-icon concept with the single mark centered.
