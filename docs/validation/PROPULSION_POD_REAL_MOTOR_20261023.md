## Winding and material admission

Native zero-distance series connectors each join two coil groups; twelve such branches pair one phase-side coil and one neutral-side coil. The unique nearest phase/neutral bus distance is 0.15 mm, versus approximately 1.2 mm for other buses. Bridging these CAD gaps is an explicit assembly assumption, not existing metal continuity. Four candidate equal branches occur per phase, each with two ten-turn coils in series. Conditional source fraction is therefore +/-i_phase/4 per physical conductor. Current source and linkage extraction use the same signed basis; net signed phase turns must vanish, preserving gauge invariance.

Independent review identified ambiguous multi-side contact samples for the first connector/coil group: arbitrary ordering/averaging of distance-point samples does not prove unique terminal topology or absence of representational shorts. Detailed native piece-path/overlap inspection is retained separately in `conductor-path-native.json`: Coil019 forms the nineteen-piece chain 0-9-1-10-...-8-17-18; Connect touches terminal piece 0 and overlaps internal return-link piece 9 by 4.49918678 mm^3. Coil039 connector touches only terminal piece 0. Literal CAD metal connectivity therefore contains a bypass/possible shorted turn; an intended datasheet-ten-turn interpretation must explicitly override that representational overlap with a connector-clearance or insulating-interface assumption. No repaired CAD or qualified ten-turn hardware continuity is claimed. The source winding helper must not be called a measured or fully qualified winding map until that gate is closed. Any candidate traversal/assembly assumption is declared explicitly.

The API requires material laws, material provenance, winding-side fractions and connection provenance; there are no silently supplied motor materials. Original H070-20 is grain-oriented; the existing kernel supports isotropic scalar permeability or isotropic convex B-H curves only. `acknowledge_isotropic_steel_approximation` must be true for this adapter. The executed illustrative case uses mu_r=200 as an explicit numerical assumption, not a fit to H070-20. [Arnold G52UH revision 210607](https://www.arnoldmagnetics.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/11/N52UH_GBDD_181031.pdf) supplies nominal Br=1.43 T and HcB=1086 kA/m, giving a candidate initial recoil mu_r near 1.048. This is a traceable supplier substitution, not identification/calibration of the actual motor magnets. Alternating tangential magnet signs are explicitly supplied; global polarity and assembly orientation require source evidence.

The official thyssenkrupp powercore product range 02/2023 lists H070-20 grade loss/polarization and physical properties but does not supply the required full directional B-H or 1 kHz loss map. No curve is fabricated from a single grade test point. 4300 rpm with fourteen pole pairs is approximately 1003 Hz; a 50 Hz loss number cannot qualify that operating point.

## Field functional and current limitations

For each admitted geometry, existing P1 magnetostatics solves stationarity of Pi(a;i,theta)=d*integral w(B,theta)dA-d*integral J(i)*a dA. For recoil PM, add the stated constant reference d*integral_PM Br^2/(2mu)dA to the kernel's zero-at-B=0 energy. Then lambda=d*C^T*a, U=constitutive energy with PM reference, Wprime=i^T*lambda-U. Full physical lambda_a/b/c is returned; zero-sequence linkage is never silently projected away. Floating-star currents must sum to zero. Nonlinear steel is admitted only through explicitly supplied kernel-supported convex B-H data; no saturation curve is inferred.

The initial implementation evaluated torque by centered angle differences of fully re-solved coenergy. Executed checks showed this is not yet a qualified torque port: independent constrained triangulation at each perturbed angle changes connectivity and amplifies discretization error. At i=(1,-1,0) A, theta=.002 rad, baseline 6728 nodes/13198 triangles gave about 2.53 N m; halving boundary target edge gave about 1.24 N m; uniform red refinement gave about 1.34 N m. Delta-angle refinement alone did not resolve the mesh discrepancy. These are retained failed convergence findings, not accepted motor torque predictions. Backend qualification freeze then paused source edits. Planned correction uses one common anchor connectivity with rigid rotor rotation and smooth air-gap mesh deformation, positive element Jacobians, and stress/shape-derivative convergence checks. Native radial bounds establish a 400 micrometer vacuum gap (rotor maximum radius 44.2 mm, stator minimum 44.6 mm); explicit virtual air mesh layers can refine this gap without altering materials. Read-only ALE prototypes already show stable angle differences but unresolved mesh/traction discrepancies. A maximum kernel-node-budget rejection on a second global red refinement is retained as a limit, not hidden as a successful run. No real-motor coupled transient is qualified by the initial field snapshot.

## Stationary-potential and volume-weighted virtual work

The main torque port now uses the exact discrete P1 shape derivative, not subtraction of independently remeshed total PM energies. At the converged central potential A, define nodal velocity u_i=w_i*J*x_i for the fixed ALE rotation weights and 90-degree rotation matrix J. On each triangle, G=grad(u)=sum_i u_i tensor grad(N_i). The transported scalar Az gives Bprime=(G-tr(G)I)B and area_prime=area*tr(G). Therefore the vacuum energy shape derivative is area/mu0*(B^T*G*B-.5*|B|^2*tr(G)); torque is minus depth times its sum. Every moving metal/PM triangle is rigidly rotated, its isotropic constitutive potential and co-rotated remanence remain invariant, and all winding/source triangles are fixed. These terms vanish for this declared map by invariance, rather than being omitted as an unsupported simplification. This does not extend to anisotropic, nonrigid or unrotated-PM material laws.

An independent reference holds the central nodal A fixed, evaluates the perturbed operators and differences vacuum element integrals before summation. This avoids cancellation of the large PM baseline and converges quadratically in angular step. Another reference re-solves the full stationary PDE at both angles. On the original coarse source, exact discrete contraction 1.602309343934 N m agrees with central-A difference at delta=2.5e-5 rad, 1.602309105067 N m, within 2.39e-7 N m. This establishes a discrete-functional identity, not physical torque accuracy.

A separate [COMSOL 6.4 primary method description, equations 3-11 and 3-12](https://doc.comsol.com/6.4/doc/com.comsol.help.acdc/acdc_ug_modeling.06.20.html) supports Maxwell torque and its cylindrical air-gap volume average. Integrating the contour torque uniformly over a vacuum annulus gives tau=d/(mu0*(b-a))*integral r*Br*Bphi dArea. The adapter computes actual triangle/annulus intersections and triangle quadrature, checking coverage and rejecting nonvacuum intersections. No centroid material selection substitutes for clipping. A manufactured source-free Br=.004/r, Bphi=.002/r annulus has exactly 1 N m for depth .025 m; joint angular/radial refinement checks the quadrature against that independent reference. A coarse polygon mesh initially failed coverage because its inscribed outer boundary did not cover the requested annulus; the manufactured domain was corrected, preserving the coverage check. Refining angular coordinates alone did not remove radial sampling error; both coordinates are refined in the actual test.

The analytic velocity u=alpha(r)*J*x with alpha=(b-r)/(b-a) on EXACTLY that same annulus gives the same integral by Cartesian stress contraction, since its skew rotation term has zero contraction with symmetric Maxwell stress. This is checked using separately formed Cartesian grad(u). Comparing that analytic profile with its P1 nodal interpolation isolates velocity interpolation/support error from actual field-stress variation across annuli; the original full-gap ALE weights have different support and need not give the same coarse discrete number.

For fixed-theta magnetostatics only, all copper and virtual air-layer regions have exactly the same mu_r=1 constitutive law. They are coalesced into one kernel material group while retaining every physical geometry interface, per-element signed chi, J source and result geometry label. Against a retained 507-group solve the optimized 31-group solve had exactly zero maximum nodal-A and full-phase-lambda differences. No such merge is applied to eddy-current regions, which carry independent circuit identity. Cached winding chi is valid because every winding material node is fixed by the checked ALE map.
