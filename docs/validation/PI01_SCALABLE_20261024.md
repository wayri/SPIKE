  components, holes, authored interfaces, full-pad patches and all unexcited

  refinement; an interior witness plus zero measured outside area avoids a

  [independent oracles](../DC_TETRA_SCALING_ORACLES.md): a separate explicit

  operator, one sparse factorization reused for conditioning and solve, positive
  symmetric-pivot and Ritz-residual screens, and per-component current/work
  checks. The old bounded profile retains its limits and inverse-mass method.
* [Batched geometry audit](../DC_GEOMETRY_SCALING.md): vectorize the same 44 SAT
  axes for bounded batches of candidates. Keep topology, hanging-node,
  separation predicates, tolerances, candidate/hierarchy counters and resource
  ceilings unchanged. The existing shared validator benefits thermal,
  electrical and structural users without introducing new physical laws.
* Supervised selected-volume runs retain raw meshes, signed face currents,
  vector fields, per-volume watts, density bounds, source/model/method hashes
  and native OS memory diagnostics. Existing process supervision enforces
  4,096 MiB, at most 600 seconds, 512 MiB files and bounded streams. The shared
  pre-import runtime helper configures one BLAS thread in the child only.

The independent thin six-tetrahedron test exposed inverse/projection symmetry
loss. The Gram form passes its analytical current, every signed face current,
cell and face potentials, heat and volume without relaxing the symmetry or
physical checks. A hidden negative-mode example also demonstrated why the
nearest-zero eigenpair alone cannot establish positivity; the symmetric-factor
screen rejects it. These remain numerical screens, not certified inertia or
condition-number bounds. Human numerical release review remains required.
