# Public equal-model ladder corpus provenance

Review date: 2026-08-30

Review scope: first-party engineering provenance and redistribution metadata;
this is not legal advice or an external legal opinion.

The three circuit decks are deterministically generated by
`scripts/generate_spikes_public_ladder_corpus.py`. They contain only a current
source, unit resistors, an operating-point directive, and comments. No vendor
models, copied example decks, measured datasets, trademarks, or third-party
numeric parameter sets are included.

The directory-specific `LICENSE.txt` applies CC0-1.0 to the generated decks.
The source and generator are retained so every deck can be regenerated and
reviewed. SHA-256 identities are computed by the executable manifest builder;
manual digest transcription is not trusted.

The size tiers are engineering scale labels:

- small: 16 resistor sections;
- medium: 500 resistor sections;
- large: 2,000 resistor sections.

These are intentionally transparent sparse-MNA scaling fixtures. They do not
represent converter, RF, compact-model, or industry-application breadth.
