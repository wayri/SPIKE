# ELP32/N97 original core geometry and property inventory

`original-nominal-elp32-core-pair.step` contains two closed, originally authored rectilinear E halves, nominally based on the TDK ELP32/6/20 drawing. It is **not manufacturer CAD** and omits undimensioned fillets/bevels/clamp recess details. STEP units are millimetres; origin is the paired-core mating plane centre. Pair bounds: x±15.875, y±10.175, z±6.35mm. Each window spans x3.175..12.7 or−12.7..−3.175 and z−3.2..3.2, open through the20.35mm depth. Core contact gap is zero nominal; manufacturing tolerance and practical mating/adhesive gap remain separate.

The candidate is TDK B66287G0000X197 N97, sold per individual half; two form an EELP32 set. Drawing review: ELP32/6/20 PDF p2, October2022. N97 review: material PDF p2, June2025. URLs, hashes and reviewed pages are retained in `source-manifest.json`. Supplier PDFs and rendered pages are excluded from the distributable pack; no supplier CAD or raster drawing is redistributed. The original generation script and STEP are Apache-2.0 under the included license.

`n97-core-material.json` is an evidence inventory, not a complete validated SPIKE solver card. It retains per-set magnetic effective geometry Ae130mm², le41.4mm, Ve5390mm³ and ungapped AL5700nH/turn²±25%. Material μi2300±25% at25°C differs from this core-set μe1440. Material Bs values atH1200A/m differ from the core-table Bs atH250A/m; conditions are preserved explicitly. Discrete typical material losses and the core-set2.60W upper limit have different meanings and are never fitted into a Steinmetz law or extrapolated to arbitrary converter waveforms.

Thermal conductivity, specific heat, Young modulus, Poisson ratio, CTE and full nonlinear B-H/frequency-temperature loss laws are **unknown** from the reviewed sources and remain null. Density4850kg/m³ and resistivity8Ωm are source properties, not sufficient to infer those missing values. No structural, thermal, saturation, loss, isolation or full3D coupled qualification is conferred by displaying this core in an assembly. Converter target48V→1.8V/50W is a project objective, not a proven rating for this geometry or winding design.

`geometry-verification.json` checks two closed solids, nominal bounds, an independent rectangular-volume expression and STEP reimport. This nominal CAD volume differs from supplier magnetic effective Ve; do not substitute one for the other. Mechanical mating/isolation clearance, construction tolerances, creepage, high-current terminals and thermal design still require their own evidence.

`original-core-pin34-module.step` is the36-solid core+pins compound shifted+6.6mm in z for carrier-top reference. Primary pins then spanz−1.6..7.4mm, secondary−1.6..9mm. It intentionally has no PCB until located winding-board STEP exports are supplied. `build_assembly.py --primary PATH --secondary PATH` imports native board+copper STEP exports with component models omitted, verifies the dominant dielectric body dimensions, and rigidly centres/locates the actual lower copper envelope at primary0mm and secondary1mm. It preserves all exported dimensions and separate solids, then exports `original-planar-module-full.step` with the same+6.6mm global transform. The actual exported copper/pad thickness is0.7900002mm versus nominal0.8mm; no scaling or mask volume is invented. Compound STEP import checks solid count and total volume. Assembly geometry does not establish pin ampacity, solder adequacy, isolation or coupled electromagnetic/thermal performance.

A separate **illustrative inert** material snapshot is provided as `generic-ferrite-project-material.json` and `generic-ferrite-material-snapshot.json`; both pass SPIKE's `validate_material`/`validate_snapshot`. Unlike the N97-specific inventory, this record explicitly selects generic approximate ferrite thermal/mechanical data from TDK General Definitions October2022 section8.1,p17: E150GPa, k5.5W/(mK) midpoint of4..7, cp700J/(kgK), CTE8.5e−6/K midpoint of7..10e−6. These are marked `estimate`, not N97-specific manufacturer values. Density4850, small-signal μ2300, and reciprocal resistivity conductivity0.125S/m retain N97 manufacturer provenance. Poisson ratio is unavailable, so a complete elastic model remains unsupported. Assignment is inert metadata and does not automatically project properties into any solver. Primary source: https://www.tdk-electronics.tdk.com/download/540066/fb53a2e55d6690c04d227a78a5be58f0/pdf-generaldefinitions.pdf . Supplier PDF is not redistributed.

Two **core-only supplied-heating** native thermal projects are also included: `core-supplied-1w.spike` and `core-reference-supplied-2p6w.spike`. Open each, choose Assembly volume field study, and inspect/run Transient solid thermal. Corresponding `.fieldstudy.json` is importable through Open volume study. Each contains25 real saved temperature frames from0..120s, 2s steps, 5s output. Both use the explicit generic k5.5/cp700 estimates, N97 density4850, exterior prescribed convection10W/(m²K), ambient298.15K and an illustrative mating-face conductance10000W/(m²K). No winding, converter, radiation, airflow or temperature-dependent core loss is solved. The two halves are symmetrically heated; the declared contact is not qualified by this symmetric run.

Input heat is **supplied**, uniformly1W or2.6W total, rather than calculated from transformer operation. The2.6W case merely exercises a numerical reference load matching the supplier set-loss ceiling at its specific100kHz/200mT/100°C condition; its evolving temperature and actual converter excitation do not meet that supplier test condition. It is not a predicted or guaranteed loss at the48V→1.8V target. Source heat is normalized by actual rectilinear CAD/tetra volume, not magnetic effective Ve. At120s peak temperature is303.745K for1W and312.696K for2.6W under these assumptions.

`thermal-verification.json` retains exact requests/results/time settings and native current-result readback; `thermal-independent-checks.json` independently integrates tetra volume/capacity storage and verifies input-minus-convection energy, plus linear temperature-rise scaling with supplied power. These checks do not replace temporal/spatial refinement or hardware validation. Imported mesh source hashes are caller declarations; STEP and mesh share original nominal dimensions, without independent automatic CAD-mesh authentication. Reproduce with `run_core_thermal.py` in this source directory; no solver code changes are involved.
