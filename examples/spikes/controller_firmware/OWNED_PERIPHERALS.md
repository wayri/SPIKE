# Owned peripheral electrical examples (experimental)

These original minimal Thumb fixtures are generated by
`scripts/make_spikes_peripheral_examples.py`. They are not vendor firmware,
HAL compatibility tests, or production controller software.

Select the STM32F407 Discovery target and **Owned PWM / SPI / I2C — fixed-clock
test profile** in electrical pin properties. Use a non-UIC transient with a
1 us reporting step, firmware-controlled outputs, actual VDD=3.3 V and VSS=0.
The profile uses a functional 1 us CPU instruction cadence, not physical CPU
cycle timing. Clocks are explicit, not inferred from RCC: timer10 MHz,
SPI input80 MHz, I2C input8 MHz. Imports do not execute until hash-specific
firmware/runtime approval. Existing saved modes are not changed automatically.

| Binary | Pin mapping and electrical fixture | Bounded expected behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `owned_pwm.bin` | PA0 AF1 → `pwm`, 1 kohm and20 pF to ground | PSC=0, ARR=3, CCR=2; period400 ns, 50% duty. Observe27 instructions. |
| `owned_spi_loopback.bin` | PA5 AF5 →`clk` with100 kohm to ground; PA7 MOSI and PA6 MISO AF5 both→`data` with100 kohm and20 pF to ground | SPI mode0,20 MHz. Eight actual electrical MISO samples return0xA5 in R2 after18 instructions; not a fabricated returned byte. |
| `owned_i2c_address.bin` | PB6 SCL and PB7 SDA AF4/open-drain→`clk`,`data`; each pulled to VDD through2.2 kohm, each20 pF to ground | Standard mode nominal100 kHz; no target attached. Actual SDA samples give NACK/AF in R2 after180 instructions. Not an ACK-target example. |

Finite output resistance, pulls, pad capacitance, package R/L and clamps are
configured through the labeled electrical pin editor. They remain supplied
generic assumptions, not fitted STM32 pad data. Set numerical timesteps and
check convergence for your actual ringing circuit; these fixtures do not
qualify high-frequency SI/PI or a PCB interconnect model.

The engine advances the owned C++ circuit to every timed peripheral barrier,
samples the accepted pre-edge left limit, then changes outputs. I2C SCL-high
recognition uses a1 ns observation grid and supports bounded clock stretching.
Reporting boundaries do not replace the sub-instruction sample barriers.

Only fixed routes and strict single-byte polled registers are admitted.
All RCC writes (including clock enables/gates), interrupts, peripheral DMA,
reset changes, other modes/routes and broad
SDK initialization are rejected. Terminal self-branches can be idle-skipped
by the functional CPU backend; the fixtures provide32 straight-line NOPs
after the work for bounded observation before that unsupported condition.
On a coupled failure restart CPU and plant together; no combined rollback is
provided. These are engineering examples, not hard-real-time/HIL certification.
